Caelo connects to a VPN using a subscription link — one address that carries every
server your provider offers, and updates itself when that list changes. Paste the
link once; there is no account to create and nothing to sign in to.

It speaks AmneziaWG: WireGuard with the packets shaped so that a handshake does
not look like a handshake. Where a connection is examined rather than merely
routed, that is the difference between working and not.

What it does not do:

• No account, no email, no identifier of any kind.
• No analytics, no crash reporting, no third-party SDKs.
• The only thing it sends on its own is a request for one static file, to ask
  whether a newer version exists. That request is identical from every
  installation, carries nothing about you, goes through the tunnel when one is
  up, and can be turned off in Settings.
• Your keys and configurations stay on the device.
